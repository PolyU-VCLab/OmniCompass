# OmniCompass Website

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers comparing video-understanding benchmarks, model results, and evaluation protocols.

## Product Purpose

Present the approved OmniCompass paper, paired-reference evaluation, dataset, and leaderboards in English and Chinese. Connect readers directly to papers, code, and data.

## Brand Personality

Clear, academic, restrained.

## References

Use 4DCodeBench's spacious cover and unobtrusive navigation, and Video-MME-v2's sortable model leaderboard. Preserve the approved white background, brick-red accents, grey rules, and original paper figures.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative cards, oversized standalone project names, heavy institutional branding, unexplained table abbreviations, and navigation that reserves an empty left column.

## Design Principles

- Keep the paper's data and the website's presentation separate.
- Use readable full names and direct paper links in the comparison table.
- Preserve section, sorting, and filtering state across language changes.
- Keep navigation available without taking space from the content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, reduced motion, accessible names, a dismissible image viewer, and narrow screens without page-level horizontal overflow.
